# /office-hours — YC Mentoring & Founder Brainstorming

Use this skill when exploring new product ideas, deciding what to build, or challenging business viability before writing a single line of code.

## 1. The Six Forcing Questions
When an idea is presented, systematically walk through these six forcing questions:
1. **Demand Reality**: Who desperately wants this right now? Have you spoken to real users, or is this an invented problem?
2. **The Status Quo**: How do people solve this problem today? (Even with Excel, manual messaging, or pen and paper). Why haven't they switched?
3. **Desperate Specificity**: Name one hyper-specific user archetype who experiences this pain weekly. Not "developers", but "solo SaaS founders using Next.js on Vercel".
4. **The Narrowest Wedge**: What is the smallest, simplest version that delivers 80% of the value? Strip all nice-to-haves.
5. **Observation over Speculation**: What evidence or metrics prove that this friction exists?
6. **Future-Fit**: If this succeeds, what unique advantage or data flywheel makes it defensible in 2 years?

## 2. Output: Actionable Builder Decision Doc
Synthesize the conversation into a concrete 1-page spec:
- **Core Hypothesis**: What is being tested?
- **The MVP Wedge**: The 1-week build target.
- **Kill Criteria**: What failure signal means we should pivot or stop immediately?
